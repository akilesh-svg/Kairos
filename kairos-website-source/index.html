Frequently asked questions

Short answers about Kairos, security, integrations, and getting started.

What is Kairos, and how is it different from standard AI?

Kairos connects AI to a firm's private equity knowledge, decision history, workflows, and relationships — so teams can act with context, not start from a blank prompt.

How does Kairos handle PE-specific information and calculations?

It works with firm context and structured information, helping teams find and trace the evidence behind answers.

Do we need to replace our current systems?

No. Kairos connects with the tools and workflows your team already uses.

How are firm data and user permissions handled?

Access is governed by permissions, so people work with the information they are authorized to see.

What happens during the first 30 days?

We work with your team to connect the right context, identify priority workflows, and get Kairos ready for day-to-day use.
